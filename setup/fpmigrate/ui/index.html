You're all set

Pick up where you left off

    Watch on your TV and phone

    Install the free Jellyfin app from your TV's or phone's app store, then sign in with the same name and password. On the same Wi-Fi it finds this server automatically.

    Use the server address from Open Jellyfin if your device does not find it automatically.

    Keep this app

    Add Orbital for Jellyfin to your Start menu to switch the look, manage sports, or undo later. No administrator prompt; remove it any time from Settings > Apps.

    Your library and requests

    These features use your existing Jellyfin library. Each person's choices stay with their own account; no extra API key is needed.

    Optional: enter your Seerr address, then copy its API key from Settings → General. Users sign into Seerr with their Jellyfin account once; Orbital discovers that link automatically and keeps Seerr's approval rules.

    Connect a service catalogue

    Already connected Seerr above? Choose a service and we will download its US title listings, check them, and connect Services on web, Phone and TV. No export file needed. This may take several minutes; progress appears below. Run this again whenever you want to refresh listings.

    Metadata from TMDB; availability powered by JustWatch. Listings do not include subscriptions or playable video. TMDB · JustWatch

    Already have a US catalogue export from your metadata source? Choose its JSON file below. We check the titles, dates and artwork paths, then connect the same Services browser on web, Phone and TV. This adds listings, not streaming subscriptions or playable video.

    Where Sports gets its games

    Sports shows the games in your own TV guide. Connect a source for scores and more; each one uses your own account, and Orbital never charges for it.

    Help me connect scores and team logos

    A sports source supplies schedules, scores and team badges. We do not include someone else's account or paid access.

    1. Open TheSportsDB's key and setup instructions. Choose the access you need; provider plans may have their own fees.
    2. Copy the key allowed for your use. Personal premium keys are on your provider profile. Treat your personal key like a password.
    3. Paste it below, then press Save and check sources. We'll save it on your server and check today's schedule.

    A successful check can return zero games today. Team badges appear only when the source supplies them; an empty schedule cannot confirm badge coverage. If access fails, the result below explains what to fix.

    You can skip this. Sports will use your TV guide, and you can connect a source later.

    Add-ons

    Sources other people wrote and shared, for example on GitHub. Orbital doesn't write or check them: before one runs you'll see who wrote it and whose data it uses.

      Free, and what's coming

      Everything you just set up is free and stays free: the move, the Orbital look, and sports from your TV guide plus any source you connect. When you watch a game on one of your channels, its tracker waits for your picture; press Sync once to line it up.

      Coming later as Orbital Plus: a tracker that locks to your picture automatically, plays read straight from the score display on your screen (pitch by pitch, with the pitch type and speed when the network shows them), and channels checked against the actual picture with automatic switching. The first 25 founding households get Plus free. Nothing here asks for a card.
