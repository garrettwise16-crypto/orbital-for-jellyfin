        Help me set up Rebel for personal use
        
    Rebel runs on your own always-on computer and organises sources you choose. It does not include a guaranteed sports package. Its personal-use terms and the terms of your sources apply.
    
        
    1. Check this computer and prepare Docker.
    2. Install Rebel, then choose sources in its dashboard.
    3. Test the channel list and guide, then save them in Jellyfin.
    
        
        
    
        
    Start with Check this computer. This step does not change anything.
    
        
    Open the official Docker Desktop installer instructions. Complete its installation and any restart, open Docker Desktop, then return and choose Check again. Docker has its own licensing terms.
    
        
    
          
    Open my Rebel dashboard. It may take a minute to start. Set your dashboard password, choose your country and sources, and let Rebel build its channel list. Keep the dashboard on this computer; remote viewing is configured through Jellyfin.
    
          
          
    If you enabled an API key, copy the complete playlist and guide links from Rebel’s Players settings into the form below, then test and save. An empty channel list means source setup still needs attention.
    
        
    
        
    Already running Rebel elsewhere? Copy its playlist and guide links into Add a live TV source below. Official setup for other computers and NAS devices. This installer keeps Rebel’s data and never replaces other containers. Update Rebel through its official instructions; Docker host-control access is not granted.

      2. Choose your address
          
          
      Open DuckDNS, sign in, and add a name you like. Copy the full name and your account token below, then come back here. Your token is used only for DuckDNS address updates and is stored encrypted on this computer.
      
          
      
          
          
          
        
      
        
      
          
      3. Connect your router
      
          
      In your router, find Port forwarding (sometimes under Advanced or NAT). Add two TCP entries: outside 80 → this computer, inside 80; outside 443 → this computer, inside 443. Do not forward 8096. Reserve this computer's local address under DHCP / Address reservation.
      
          
      If your router is managed through an ISP app, make those entries there. We never need your router password. If your ISP blocks incoming connections or uses a shared address, choose the private connection above.

      Where Sports gets its games
      
      
      Sports shows the games in your own TV guide. Connect a source for scores and more; each one uses your own account, and Orbital never charges for it.
      
      
      
      
      Help me connect scores and team logos
        
      A sports source supplies schedules, scores and team badges. We do not include someone else's account or paid access.
      
        
      1. Open TheSportsDB's key and setup instructions. Choose the access you need; provider plans may have their own fees.
      2. Copy the key allowed for your use. Personal premium keys are on your provider profile. Treat your personal key like a password.
      3. Paste it below, then press Save and check sources. We'll save it on your server and check today's schedule.
      
        
      A successful check can return zero games today. Team badges appear only when the source supplies them; an empty schedule cannot confirm badge coverage. If access fails, the result below explains what to fix.
      
        
      You can skip this. Sports will use your TV guide, and you can connect a source later.

        Free, and what's coming
      
        Everything you just set up is free and stays free: the move, the Orbital look, and sports from your TV guide plus any source you connect. When you watch a game on one of your channels, its tracker waits for your picture; press Sync once to line it up.
        
      
        Coming later as Orbital Plus: a tracker that locks to your picture automatically, plays read straight from the score display on your screen (pitch by pitch, with the pitch type and speed when the network shows them), and channels checked against the actual picture with automatic switching. The first 25 founding households get Plus free. Nothing here asks for a card.
