# Free and Orbital Plus

Everything in this repository is free and stays free: the web interface, sports-hub, game centers, play trackers and favorite teams. Nothing in it is locked, time-limited or waiting for a key.

**Orbital Plus** is an optional paid add-on that will come later. It is **not in this repository** and isn't available yet.

## What's free (this repository)

- The full web interface: Home, libraries, search, Live TV, guide and Sports.
- sports-hub: schedules, live scores, game centers, play trackers, pregame previews, rankings, team pages and favorite teams.
- **Tracker for the game you're watching:** live TV usually runs behind real time, so the tracker waits by an estimated delay to avoid showing a play before you see it. Press **Sync** to line it up with your picture. Adjust it any time.

## What Orbital Plus is planned to add

- **Picture-timed tracker:** the tracker follows the picture you are actually watching, so each pitch or play appears as it happens on your screen, without pressing Sync.
- **Feed checks:** a check that a channel is showing the right game before you pick it.

Details, pricing and timing are not decided. This page will be updated when there is something to try.

## How the two fit together

- The free version contains no Plus code, no license checks and nothing that contacts a payment service.
- Where Plus would plug in, the free version uses its own built-in behavior (the estimated delay and Sync). Removing or never adding Plus changes nothing else.
- Plus will never be required to use anything in this repository.
